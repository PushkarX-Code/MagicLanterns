# Microsoft Store

Everything needed to list Magic Lanterns, ready to paste into Partner Center.

## One-time setup (you)

1. Create a Partner Center account at https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard (individual developer). Verify your identity.
2. **Apps and games → New product → MSIX or PWA app**, reserve the name **Magic Lanterns**.
3. **Product identity** page: copy three values into this repo's **Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions → Variables**:
   - `STORE_IDENTITY_NAME` ← *Package/Identity/Name*
   - `STORE_PUBLISHER` ← *Package/Identity/Publisher* (starts with `CN=`)
   - `STORE_PUBLISHER_NAME` ← *Package/Properties/PublisherDisplayName*
4. Run **Actions → Release** with the next version. Download `MagicLanterns-Store.appx` from the run's artifacts and upload it under **Packages**.
5. **Privacy policy URL**: https://magiclanterns.netlify.app/privacy.html
6. Complete the age rating questionnaire (no user interaction, no data sharing, no purchases yet → rated for everyone).

The Store signs the package, so Store installs never show the "Windows protected your PC" warning. Store copies are updated by the Store; the app's own updater switches itself off there.

## Listing

**Name:** Magic Lanterns

**Short description (≤ 100 characters):**
Your films, presented like a cinema. A beautiful player for the movies and shows on your PC.

**Description:**
Magic Lanterns turns the films and shows on your computer into a library that feels like a streaming service you own: no accounts, no ads, nothing leaving your PC.

Add a folder and watch it come alive. Every film gets its poster, banner, logo, cast and ratings. Shows are grouped into seasons, each with its own cover and every episode with its own picture. Collections build themselves, from film series to a director's work.

Everything plays. MKV, MP4, AVI, HEVC, 10-bit anime releases, Dolby and DTS sound, HDR: files Windows can't play are converted on the fly using your graphics card, with seeking that's instant.

Made for watching:
• Skip Intro that finds each show's opening by listening, no one has to mark it
• Up Next, resume where you stopped, subtitles from the file or beside it, in your style
• Search that forgives typos, cast pages with everything an actor is in
• Top 10 This Week and where to watch what you don't own
• A player with scrub previews, audio tracks, equaliser and snapshots
• Light and dark themes, full keyboard and controller-friendly navigation

**What's new (1.0):** The first release. Welcome to the cinema.

**Search terms (up to 7):** video player, media player, movie library, mkv player, anime player, home theater, movie organizer

**Category:** Multimedia design → Music & video (Video players)

**Screenshots:** use `docs/showcase/*.webp` exported as 1920×1080 PNG (Home, Attack on Titan, Blade Runner 2049, Seasons, Player, Light theme).
