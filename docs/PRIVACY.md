# Magic Lanterns privacy policy

*Last updated: 15 October 2026*

Magic Lanterns is a video player for the films and shows on your own computer. It is built so that your library stays yours.

## What stays on your computer

- Your films, shows, folders and file names.
- What you watch, where you stopped, your favourites, My List and every setting.
- Pictures and details saved for your library.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

## What leaves your computer, and why

To show posters, banners, cast and ratings, Magic Lanterns looks up a title's name and year with **The Movie Database (TMDB)** and, for ratings, the **OMDb API**, through our own small relay server that holds the access keys. These lookups contain only the title being matched, never your file paths, viewing history or any personal information. The relay keeps no record of who asked.

The Discover rows and "Where to watch" ask TMDB which services carry a title in your country (provided by JustWatch through TMDB). Your country comes from your computer's region setting.

The direct-download version checks GitHub once at launch for a newer version. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store.

Links you choose to open (a trailer, a streaming service, the Ko-fi page) open in your web browser, under that site's own privacy policy.

## Children

Magic Lanterns does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/PushkarX-Code/MagicLanterns-Releases/issues.
